# Contributing

This is a mixed-license repository. By submitting a contribution, you confirm that you have the right to provide it under the applicable terms below.

- Original code, scripts, tools, tests, and documentation are contributed under the MIT License in `LICENSE-CODE`.
- Original Russian translation contributions are contributed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International in `LICENSE-TRANSLATION`.
- Identify all copied or adapted material and document its source, authorship, license, and permission.
- Do not submit third-party translations, game binaries, extracted game assets, fonts, or other copyrighted material without documented permission.
- Do not remove existing attribution or imply that repository licenses cover inherited plugin code, inherited translations, Nuclear Option source text, or third-party assets.

For translation contributions, identify the English source key, author, origin, and applicable permission. Distinguish new independently authored translations from corrections or adaptations of existing translations. Small wording changes do not establish independent authorship or remove third-party rights. Preserve technical placeholders, formatting tags, whitespace-sensitive keys, and protected model and command identifiers.

When provenance is uncertain, open a discussion before submitting the material.
